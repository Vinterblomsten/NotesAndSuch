# Brain Ventricles and CFS   (NeuroFys)

Tags: #Neuroscience #Neuroanatomy #Histology 

The brain is one big soft mess, but it keeps it shape in the cranium as a result of the suspension in the CSF (**Cerebrospinal Fluid**).

The brain has a system of spaces where CFS flows, beside around the brain
![Pasted image 20260831144654](z-Figures/Pasted%20image%2020260831144654.png)

The three brain **meninges** (Hinder)
- **Dura Mater** - Collagen rich membrane
- **Arachnoid Mater** - Forming the subarachnoid space, where CSF flows, and which allows blunt force trauma
- **Pia Mater** - Soft, following the curves of the brain
![448](z-Figures/Pasted%20image%2020260831145108.png)
**Ependyma**
The lining of the ventricle system and the central canal of the spinal chord. Formed by [ependymacytotes](Cells%20of%20the%20Nervous%20System%20-%20%28NeuroFys%29.md) they **don't** form tight junctions in contrast to the epenndymacytotes in the choroid plexus.

**Choroid plexus**
On the tela choroidea (region of pia mater in the ventricles). 
![346](z-Figures/Screenshot%202026-09-01%20at%2021.03.26.png)

The choroid plexus consists of **modified ependymocytes** surrounding a core of capillaries and connective tissues. Here **they form tight junctions**, and thus form the [blood-CSF barrier](Blood-Brain-Barrier%20%28NeuroFys%29.md). 
They produce and secretes the CSF by **filtering plasma from the blood**.
