- This phenomenom is replicated with octopi in a japanese experiment

#### Neural models and representations
- Broadmann areas - separated by the microstructural compositions of regions
- (Single) Dissociation studies show cognitive functions somewhat tied to different regions of the brain
- Double dissociation studies show actual seperation of functions

- However, imaging shows a great mix of areas for different cognitive functions, and vice versa

**Representation of cognitive functions**
- Receptive fields - Hubel & Wiesel - The cognitive function of object recognitions is represented at the most basic levels of single neurons carrying representations of edges etc.
- Finger representation in somatosensory cortex - specific parts of S1 is tied to stimulation of different fingers
- Representation of working memory and action selection

**How do we understand cognitive functions? - Mars levels of analysis**
- Computational theory - What is the goal of the computation
- Representation and algorithm - How can this computational theory be implemented algorithmically (Computer or biological network not important)
- Hardware implementation - How is it physically plausible
