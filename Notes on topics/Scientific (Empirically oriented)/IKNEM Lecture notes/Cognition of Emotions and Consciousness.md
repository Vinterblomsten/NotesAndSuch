Specifikke koncepter i forskellige kulturer
- "Gigil" (Filipino): The urge to hug or squeeze something that is unbearably adorable.
- "Voorpret" (Dutch): Pleasure felt about an event before the event takes place.
- "Age-otori" (Japanese): The feeling of looking worse after a haircut.
- “Fago” (Ifaluk, Micronesian): Can mean love, empathy, pity, sadness, or compassion, depending on context
- “Litost” (Czech): Said to be untranslatable but roughly “torment over one’s own misery combined with the desire for revenge.”
- “Arigata-meiwaku” (Japanese): felt when someone has done you a favor that you didn’t want from them, and which may have caused difficulty for you, but you’re required to be grateful any-way.
