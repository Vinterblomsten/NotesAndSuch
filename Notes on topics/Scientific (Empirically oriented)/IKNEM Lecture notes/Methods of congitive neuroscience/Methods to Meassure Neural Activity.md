#ExperimentalMethods #Neuroscience 

Some methods isolate the activity of single neurons, while others measure the activity of groups of neurons, asking how their firing patterns interact with one another.

##### Single-Cell Neurophysiology
Single-cell recording enables neurophysiologists to record from individual neurons and correlate increases and decreases in neuronal activity with sensory stimulation or behavior. With multi unit recording, we can record the activity of many neurons at the same time.

###### Electrocorticography (ECoG)
A grid of elecetrodes is placed directly on the surfuce of the brain, either outside the [[Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)#Dura|dura]] or beneath it. ###### Functional Near Infrared Optical Imaging Systems (fNIR)
Functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy (fNIRS) is a non-invasive neuroimaging technique that measures brain activity by detecting changes in blood oxygenation using near-infrared light. It works by emitting light through the scalp and measuring the amount of light absorbed by oxygenated and deoxygenated hemoglobin, which reflects neural activity. fNIRS is portable, cost-effective, and relatively easy to use, making it suitable for studying brain function in natural settings or with populations where traditional imaging methods like fMRI are impractical, such as infants, children, or individuals with mobility limitations. While it offers good temporal resolution and is less sensitive to movement artifacts, its spatial resolution is limited, and it cannot penetrate deep brain structures. fNIRS is widely used in cognitive neuroscience, clinical research, and brain-computer interface applications.
