#ExperimentalMethods 

Undesøgelser af korelation og kausalitet, eller sammenhængsforklaringer inden for kognitive og neurovidenskabelige eksperimenter

Få kritiske elementer af en kompleks naturlig situation uddrages og undersøges i laboratoriet under "rene forhold" - systematisk uden forstyrrende elementer
###### **Randomized Clinical Trial (RCT)**
Randomally selected subjects divided randomly into a test group and a control group undergo cognitive experiments with or without experimental manipulation and we den do analysis on the differences in data. 
The experimental manipulation is thoroughly though out  in terms of
- Differences in test subjetcs
- The order of the experimental manipulation
- The magnitude or levels of the experimental manipulation
