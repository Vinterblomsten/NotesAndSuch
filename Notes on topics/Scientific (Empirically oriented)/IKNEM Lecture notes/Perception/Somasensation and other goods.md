##### Somatosensory cortex
Located behind the central sulcus, is is latterally divided in the different locations of sensation, which differs in area in the cortex depending on the importance of fine tuned sense at the ares (large areas for face and hands, but small ares for the torso and back).

 ![[Screenshot 2025-02-17 at 11.28.01.png|400]]

##### Interoception
A lot of different types:
- **Chemo-receptors**: Sensitive to $CO_2$ - regulating cardiovascular effects and repiratory system - Chocking feeling
- **Central and periphery osmosis-receptors**: Sensitive to the local osmotic pressure in and outside of cells - Feeling of thirst
- **Hormonelle receptors**: Hunger, ..
- **Specialized mechano-receptors**: Blood pressure
