#Perception
##### Multi-sensory integration
**Ventriloquisms** 
 - The production of the voice in such a way that the sound seems to come from a source other than the vocal organs of the speaker 
 
**McGurk effect**
- Categorical perception (in language perception) is a product of multi-sensory integration
- In incongruent condition, perceived phoneme is a mixture of auditory and visual input
