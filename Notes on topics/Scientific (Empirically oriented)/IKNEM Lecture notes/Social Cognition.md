#Cognition 

What makes humans unique?
**General intelligence hypothesis:** Many cognitive operations more efficient
- Greater memory
- Faster learning
- Problem-solving

**Adapted intelligence hypothesis:** Cognitive abilities evolve ine response to specific environmental challenges
- Homing pigeons: Good spatial navigation
- Caching birds: Good memory skill
- Primates: Ecological intelligence hypothesis -> social intelligence hypothesis -> Cultural intelligence hypothesis

**Cultural intelligence hypothesis**: Humans are not simply social but **ultra-social** 
- Primates in general: Cooperation, collaboration
- Humans: Societies, artifacts, symbols, social practices, institutions

The human cognition relies on participating in cultural interaction (Adapted intelligence hypothesis)
- Language
- Participation in establishes practices
- Teaching and learning from each other

The result / requirement for these challenges is a specialized **social-cognition**
