##### Long term depression
- "Use it or lose it"- principle
- Long term low frequency stimulation from presynaptic neuron
- a slow, gradual release of $Ca^{2+}$ creating a slow rise in calciumlevel in the postsynaptic cell - the calciumlevel does not cross the calcium threshold to start LTP resulting in **LTD**
- The **function of LTD** is as a
	- Cellular storage mechanicsm
	- Celluar adjustment machanism
	- Mechanism to preserve the network and maintain the homeostasis

- Change in communication in synapses (increased or decreased)
- A combination

###### Non-declarative (Implicit memory)

##### Episodic vs semantic memory
![[Screenshot 2025-03-10 at 11.57.23.png|500]]

**Episodic memory**
- Personal events with the ability the recall the "scene"
- Geographic connections
- Mental schematics
- Subjective time
- Self-reference (I like ratatouille)

**Semantic memory**
- Factual knowledge
- Abstracting knowledge from many episodes of learning
	- In that way there is an overlap with the episodic memory, as the saemantic memory relies on the episodic memories to abstract knowledge
- Formed by language and cultue
