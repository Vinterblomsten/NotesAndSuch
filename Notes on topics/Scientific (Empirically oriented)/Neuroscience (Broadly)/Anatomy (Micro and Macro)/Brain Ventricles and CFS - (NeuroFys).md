The brain is one big soft mess, but it keeps it shape in the cranium as a result of the suspension in the CSF (**Cerebrospinal Fluid**).

The brain has a system of spaces where CFS flows, beside around the brain
![[Pasted image 20260831144654.png]]

The three brain **meninges** (Hinder)
- **Dura Mater** - Collagen rich membrane
- **Arachnoid Mater** - Forming the subarachnoid space, where CSF flows, and which allows blunt force trauma
- **Pia Mater** - Soft, following the curves of the brain
![[Pasted image 20260831145108.png|448]]
**Ependyma**
The lining of the ventricle system and the central canal of the spinal chord. Formed by [[Cells of the Nervous System - (NeuroFys)| ependymacytotes]] they **don't** form tight junctions in contrast to the epenndymacytotes in the choroid plexus.

**Choroid plexus**
On the tela choroidea (region of pia mater in the ventricles). 
![[Screenshot 2026-09-01 at 21.03.26.png|346]]

The choroid plexus consists of **modified ependymocytes** surrounding a core of capillaries and connective tissues. Here **they form tight junctions**, and thus form the [[Blood-Brain-Barrier (NeuroFys)|blood-CSF barrier]]. 
They produce and secretes the CSF by **filtering plasma from the blood**.
