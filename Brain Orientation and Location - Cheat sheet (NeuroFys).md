# Brain Orientation and Location   Cheat sheet (NeuroFys)

Tags: #Neuroanatomy 

1. **Sagittal Plane**: The plane that divides the body into right and left halves.
2. **Coronal Plane (Frontal/Transverse Plane)**: The plane that divides the body into front (anterior) and back (posterior) parts.
3. **Horizontal Plane**: The plane that divides the brain into upper and lower parts.
4. **Medial**: Toward the middle of the body.
5. **Lateral**: Toward the side of the body.
6. **Ipsilateral**: On the same side of the body.
7. **Contralateral**: On the opposite side of the body.
8. **Superior**: Above or higher in position.
9. **Inferior**: Below or lower in position.
10. **Anterior (Rostral)**: Toward the head end or front of the body.
11. **Posterior (Caudal, c)**: Toward the tail end or back of the body.
12. **Proximal**: Near the center or point of attachment.
13. **Distal**: Toward the periphery or end of a limb.
14. **Afferent**: Carrying information into a region of interest.
15. **Efferent**: Carrying information away from a region of interest.
16. **Dorsal**: Toward or at the back (or top of the brain in humans).
17. **Ventral**: Toward the belly (or bottom of the brain in humans).
18. **Orbito**: Towards the eyes (underside of PFC)
