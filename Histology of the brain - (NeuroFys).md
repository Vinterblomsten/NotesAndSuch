# Histology of the brain   (NeuroFys)

Tags: #Neuroscience #Histology

When inspecting brainmatter it must be cut in thin slices and stained with a variety of techniques to observe the cells. There exists quite a lot of **staining methods**

**HE stain**
- Staining with heamatoxylin and eosin
- Cell Nuclei and Nissl-substance stained blue-purple by heamatoxylin
- Cytoplasm and extracellular matrix is stained pink

**Nissl stain**
- Nissl-substance (RNA and ER complex) is coloured violet/blue especially, which allows to indentify the axon hillock, which has minimal nissl-substance
- The nucleus of the granula cells are coloured especially in the cerebellum 

**Golgi stain**
- Staining with metal salts (Silver/gold)
- Largely unknown why it works, but it stains a **limited number of cells at random**, inlcuding **dendrites** and **soma**, allowing connections to be observed

**Myeling stain (Klüver-Barrera)**
- Using Luxol fast blue 
- Stains the white matter via **myelin being stained**
- The mechanism involves binding to the basic parts of the phospholipids of the myelin sheets

**Weigert Stain** - Simillar to Klüver-Barrera??

**Neurotransmitters**
Without staining, areas with large amounts of dopamine (e.g. in the substantia nigra) and adrenalin can be seen as a darker color

**Immunehistochemical staining**
By creating antibodies with stainable molecules **directing the enzymes** responsible for catalyzing the neurotransmitters
